Privacy Policy

Kano Rclone is a private, personal-use application for managing files stored in Google Drive using Rclone.

Data access and use

The application accesses Google Drive files only to perform file management operations requested by its authorized user.

Data storage and protection

Files may be temporarily cached on the user's private server. OAuth credentials are stored locally with restricted permissions.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI model training.

Data deletion

The user can remove local cached data and revoke Google Drive access through their Google Account settings.
